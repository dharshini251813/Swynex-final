A Letter for My Anna 💌

Dear Anna, ❤️

I don't really know where to begin, because when it comes to thanking you, words never seem enough.

You are not my brother by blood, but that doesn't make the bond we share any less meaningful to me. Sometimes, family isn't just about being born into the same home. Sometimes, it is about the people who come into our lives and slowly earn a special place in our hearts.

And you have your own special place in mine.

Thank you for being a part of my life. Thank you for every little thing you have done, every moment of care, every bit of support, and every time your presence has meant more to me than I could explain.

Maybe I haven't thanked you properly. Maybe I don't always express what I feel. But please don't think that means I don't value you. Some feelings are so genuine that putting them into words feels impossible.

To me, you are not just someone I call Anna. You are someone I am genuinely grateful to have met. I respect you, I value our bond, and I hope life gives you all the happiness, peace, success, and love that you deserve.

No matter how busy life gets or how much things change, I will always be thankful that our paths crossed. Being family by heart is special in its own way, and I never want to take that for granted.

I cannot explain everything in words, Anna. So, for now, let these simple words say what my heart wants you to know:

Thank you for everything. Thank you for being my Anna. I am genuinely grateful for you. ❤️

With love and respect,
Your little sister,
Dharshini 💗
